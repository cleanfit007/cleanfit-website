What is CleanFit?

CleanFit is a movement-based method that teaches you how to move better during everyday tasks — especially household cleaning. Most people rush through chores without thinking about how they're moving. Over time, that adds up.

Protect Your Body

Learn to clean without straining your back, shoulders or knees.

Move With Awareness

Build posture and mindfulness into the routines you already do every day.

Build Strength Naturally

Use daily activity as training — no extra time, no gym needed.

Develop Lasting Habits

Once you learn the method, better movement becomes second nature.

Three stages. All from your home. No extra time.

Meet the Founders

CleanFit was built by two women who noticed that everyday cleaning takes a toll on the body — and decided there had to be a better way.

Keren Orkabi

Founder

At 43, after losing her job, Keren found herself cleaning 15 hours a week. Instead of pushing through the pain, she asked: what if cleaning could build you up instead of breaking you down?

Keren Green

Co-Founder

With 25 years in the fitness industry — training everyone from teenagers to celebrities — Keren brings deep expertise in functional movement, strength, and mobility.
