Common Questions

Do I need any fitness experience?

No. CleanFit is designed for everyday people who clean their homes. No gym background needed — the movements are taught from scratch and applied to tasks you already do.

What is Skool?

Skool is a community and course platform — like a private Facebook group combined with a course library. CleanFit uses Skool to host the videos, the community, and all course content. It's free to create a Skool account.

Can I start with the free tier and upgrade later?

Yes. The free tier is a genuine starting point — not a tease. Learn the core movements, experience the method, and upgrade to the paid tier whenever you're ready for more.

What is Founder Pricing?

$9/month is the launch price for early members who join during the founding period. This rate is locked in for as long as you remain a member — the price will increase for new members joining later.

How much time does it take?

No extra time at all. You apply CleanFit during the cleaning you already do. The intro videos are under 2 minutes each. There's no separate workout — the method integrates into your existing routine.
